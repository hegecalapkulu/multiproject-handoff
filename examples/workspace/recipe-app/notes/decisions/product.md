# Decisions: product

- **v1 has no social features** (2026-11-18). Why: the goal is a tool for one household, not a network.
- **Shared shopping list is the v1 headline feature** (2026-11-22). Why: both competitors lack it and 2 of 3 interviewed households asked for it unprompted (see [interview 2026-11-21](../knowledge/interviews/2026-11-21-household-1.md) and [competitors](../knowledge/competitors/_overview.md)).
